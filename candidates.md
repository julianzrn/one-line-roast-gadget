# One-Line Roast: The Gadget You Use Every Day — 要求与候选

## 要求（源自官网 description，2026-11-25 核对）
- 一行原创吐槽，至多 200 字符。
- 对象：每天都在用的 gadget 或 app（如四点就没电的手机、开机先求好评的 app、要先升级固件的智能水壶）。
- 评审维度：好笑程度、角度新鲜、措辞干净利落。

## 候选
1. My smart kettle needs a firmware update to boil water, a substance that has been boiling successfully for billions of years without Wi-Fi. ✅ 选定（138 字符）
2. My phone dies at 4 p.m. every day like it's unionized, yet stays up all night listening for mattress ads.
3. My smartwatch charges me nine dollars a month to tell me I slept badly, as if I hadn't noticed at 3 a.m. with both eyes open.
4. The app asked me to rate it before it would open, so I did: one star, would be interrupted again.
5. My phone's battery health is 78%, its screen is cracked, and it still has the nerve to suggest I limit my screen time.
6. My fitness tracker congratulated me on 10,000 steps during a flight delay, walking to a gate that kept moving further away.
7. My doorbell camera charges a monthly subscription to tell me who was at my door, like a bouncer with a paywall.
8. My TV updated overnight, and now the volume button lives in a submenu, guarded by a loading spinner and a terms-of-service.
9. My robot vacuum got stuck under the couch, cried for help, and has been in therapy (the charging dock) ever since.

## 选定理由
候选 1：把"烧水"这个亿万年无需联网的动作和 firmware update 对撞，荒诞感最强、画面具体；"固件更新"类吐槽虽多，但落点在"水已成功沸腾几十亿年"这一句上不算撞梗。节奏：长句一气呵成，反差在尾巴上爆开。138 字符合规。
